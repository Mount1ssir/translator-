# Third-party notices

## Offline dictionary

The bundled SQLite asset contains definitions and forms from contributors to Simple English Wiktionary and English Wiktionary. The corresponding wiki entry links in WordBridge provide contributor attribution through the page history. The asset filters the source data to English entries, combines the editions, normalizes lookup keys, and indexes the resulting definitions and forms. The dataset retains its applicable Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike terms.

- Simple English Wiktionary source entries and history. [Simple English Wiktionary](https://simple.wiktionary.org/) · [Kaikki/Wiktextract export and license](https://kaikki.org/simplewiktionary/)
- English Wiktionary source entries and history. [English Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/) · [Compact Wiktionary export and license](https://github.com/tdulcet/compact-dictionaries#wiktionary)
- The upstream exports identify CC BY-SA and GFDL licensing. The compact export identifies CC BY-SA 3.0. [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) · [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) · [GFDL](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/fdl-1.3.html)

See [Dictionary data](README.md#dictionary-data) for the export URLs, checksums, and rebuild command.
